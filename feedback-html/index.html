Need help right now?

This form is for feedback, not emergencies. If someone is hurt or in danger at a site, call 1122 for rescue or 15 for police.

Common questions

Will I get a reply?

If you leave an email or phone number and tick the reply box, the team can contact you.

Can I report a damaged monument?

Yes. Choose "Complaint", pick the place, and add a photo so the team can find the spot.

Is my feedback public?

No. Your name and contact details stay with the team.

Tips for useful feedback

Name the place and the spot, add the date of your visit, and attach a photo if something needs repair. This helps the team act faster.
